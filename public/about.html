Overview

Good ideas shouldn't die for lack of a maker.

Evol started with a simple wish. We wanted a place where a student could learn electronics, bring a half-formed idea, and meet someone who could help build it.

Many good hardware ideas stall because nobody nearby knows how to build them, and many talented makers never get asked. Evol connects the two. You learn the basics with us, you tell us what you wish existed, and a maker takes it from there.

We are a student-run team in Chennai, starting with live classes inside our college. Requests, a marketplace for makers and a shop for ready-made things come next. We are building it step by step, and we would love your company along the way.

Later

How a request works

From a rough idea to something in your hands.

Requests open after our first learning batch. This is how they will run.

  1. Describe it

    Tell us the problem, your budget and when you need it. A short form keeps it clear.

  2. Makers reply

    Makers tell you how they would build it, what it costs and how long it takes.

  3. Pick and pay safely

    You choose a maker. Your payment is held and released as you approve each step.

  4. Receive it

    Your build is packed and shipped through a courier partner, with tracking.
